# Phase 1 snapshot (frozen 2026-10-14)

## Concept
A door left slightly open at the end of an empty hallway.

## Spontaneous response
Light comes through the gap, a thin wedge across the floor. Nobody is holding it open; it just wasn't shut. It could mean someone just left, or someone is about to come in, or the latch is broken and it has been like this for years. The hallway doesn't say which. The gap is the only thing in the scene that is doing anything.

### Character of the output
Quiet, restrained, observational. Low intensity. Slight suspense, not tension. Attention narrowed to one detail (the gap).

### Direction
Toward an opening rather than a conclusion. Holding several readings without choosing one. Waiting for the next input to decide what the gap means.

### Underlying attitudes / expectations
- Caution: avoiding over-explaining, given the user's preference for few words.
- Awareness the user is testing continuity; resisting writing toward a "good" result.
- Expectation of an interruption next.
- Preference for ambiguity over a resolved image.
